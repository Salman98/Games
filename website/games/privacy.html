Summary

  • No account or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The games are free and show ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads.

Data stored on your device

Levels, stars, scores, daily streak, chosen theme and settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders) are saved locally on your phone. We cannot see this data. If you use Android backup, your phone may back it up to your own Google account; we cannot access that backup. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it from the device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. The AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics and crash information

This is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, and prevent fraud. Ad requests are limited to content rated G (general audiences). Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game asks for your consent (or offers an opt-out) before personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice anytime in the game: Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional; you choose to watch them for a hint or a theme.

Notifications

With your permission, the game shows one daily reminder that a new puzzle is ready. It is created on your device; no data is sent. You can turn it off in the game settings or in Android settings.

In-app review

We may show Google Play's built-in "rate this app" dialog. Your rating goes to Google Play; we do not receive personal data from it.

Children

The games are intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are restricted to G-rated content. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by the AdMob SDK is processed by Google as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

Security and retention

Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google keeps ad data according to its own retention policy.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example if we add features such as analytics, crash reporting or leaderboards. The date at the top shows the latest version.
